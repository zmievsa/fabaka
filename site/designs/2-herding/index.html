Fabaka GitHub

A better ⌘Tab for Mac.

Hold ⌘ and press Tab: every window is listed, most recent first, 1.6 ms later. Let go, and the one you picked is on top in 50 ms.1

brew install --cask zmievsa/tap/fabaka

Needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon.

Accessibility permission only. Never Screen Recording: icons and titles, no window thumbnails.

Free and open source, MIT. No account, no telemetry, no menu-bar icon.

1 Medians from the author's M3 Pro over 617 presses in a week. At the 95th percentile the list takes 16 ms.
